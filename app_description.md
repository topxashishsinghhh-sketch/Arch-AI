**Arch – Your Privacy-First Hands-Free Voice Assistant**

Arch is a fast, secure, and privacy-focused Android voice assistant that puts you in complete control. No tracking, no cookies, no data mining - just your voice, your device, your way.

### Core Principles

- **Zero Tracking** - No analytics, cookies, or third-party trackers. Ever.
- **Local-First** - Most commands run entirely on your device without sending data anywhere.
- **Minimal Data** - Only transcripts needed for AI responses leave your device, and only when necessary.
- **Full Control** - You decide what gets saved, and you can delete everything instantly.

### Key Features

- **Hands-Free Operation** - Activate with "Hey Arch" or tap the mic. Works in the background with screen off.
- **Smart Device Control** - Make calls, send messages, open apps, set alarms/timers, control flashlight, adjust volume, and access settings.
- **Natural Conversations** - Human-like responses optimized for speaking aloud, just like Siri or Gemini.
- **Intelligent Routing** - On-device commands execute instantly offline. Complex queries go to AI only when needed.
- **Voice Confirmation** - Sensitive actions like calls and messages always require explicit voice confirmation.
- **Offline Capable** - Control your device even without internet. You'll be notified when connectivity is needed.
- **Encrypted & Local Storage** - API keys stored securely on-device. Conversation history (if enabled) stays in your device's encrypted database.

### Privacy Guarantees

- **No Audio Storage** - Your voice is processed in real-time and never recorded or saved
- **No Data Selling** - We never sell, share, or monetize your personal data
- **Transparent Processing** - Clear disclosure of what data is sent and when
- **Right to Delete** - One-tap deletion of all local data anytime
- **No Hidden Tracking** - Open about permissions and data usage

**Speak freely. Stay private. Arch gets it done.**
